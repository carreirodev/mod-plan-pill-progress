## Gaps (ranked)

1. **C15's arrow floor moved from 46 to 47 columns without being recorded.** The decision record says the row holds from 46 columns with the arrow (task Sources, Unresolved #7, design Key decision 3). The code and C15 use 47. A probe at HEAD with an agent strip showed the gap. At 46 columns the room is `floor(46 x 6.5)` = 299 px, but the bar clamps to 120 and 120 + 180 = 300 px, so the row runs past the band. At 47 columns the row fits (125 + 180 = 305). C15 follows the rule in Key decision 3: "the row holds wherever the minimum bar and the rest fit", which at 6.5 px gives 300 / 6.5 = 46.15, so 47. The "46" in the documents is an arithmetic slip. Still, the checklist changed a number from the record of decision without asking, and the handoff records "nothing new" decided. Needs a user decision: accept 47 and correct the design and task text, or trim 1 px from the reserve or the minimum so that 46 fits.
2. **VS Code is covered by width bounds only.** Criterion 1 says "desktop or VS Code", while C1 says "No desktop". Every picture proof (C1-C14, C18-C22) renders the `desktop` surface. On `vscode`, only C15 runs, plus `more_agents_line_fits_a_narrow_strip`. C15 is an upper bound only: a VS Code bar stuck at 120 px would pass it, and nothing automated checks that the bar takes the slack there (Key decision 2) or that the pill names its stage there. The risk is low because the code path is shared: only `total` branches on `e.surface` (`register.tsx:3848`), and the title-less row comes from `{Svg ? null : <Text ...>{p.title}</Text>}` (`:3906-3907`), which both surfaces take. That leaves C17 (manual) as the only cover for the headline surface.
3. **C23's "today's speed" is checked only as a ratio.** The proofs assert `running > 0` and rest = 2 x running. Doubling both speeds would pass. The speed is in fact unchanged: on both builds a 233-column loop takes 11.65 s running and 23.3 s at rest, which is 20 columns per second, and `STREAM_SPEED = 20` is untouched (`register.tsx:503`). But no assertion pins it.
4. **C2 loosens criterion 2's "12 px each side".** The text starts at x = 12, but the zone may be up to 15% wider than the Chromium-measured title plus 24. For "Refactor payments" (101.7 px measured, zone 132) the right margin comes out at about 18 px. The `MEASURED` literals (26.6, 101.7) come from a Chromium measurement this verifier could not reproduce.
5. **Rewrites go beyond what the change list declares.** All three are listed in the table above: the `fades` flip and "24 laid" becoming ">= 20" in `fill_stream_still_dots_wave_and_flicker`, the 6 px glide-back tolerance, and the `more_agents...` ellipsis keyed to the observed width. All are low severity, and none touches a case outside the list.
6. **Some checks are narrower than their criteria.** All low:
   - C3 shows the whole title in the tip only "in a bar that holds the tip". This is an author decision recorded under "Decisões tomadas na construção", not one by the user.
   - C11 drops criterion 11's "cut with … if needed, and the step after it if it fits". Pre-existing cases still pass on that behaviour (`pill_names_stage_and_step`, `long_pill_labels_keep_the_pill_to_320_px`, `cut_labels_keep_emoji_whole`). But this diff moved the pill's width cap from `W * 0.55` to `run * 0.55`, and no case exercises a narrow progress with a long name.
   - C18 adds a ">= 90 columns of fill" precondition to criterion 18's "Sempre".
   - C9 reads "green" off the pill rect alone.
7. **Expected values read back from the build.** C5-C8, C14, C18 and C19 compute the zone from the build's own fill head, not from a literal. Two cases anchor the absolute zone independently: C2 (Chromium-measured title widths) and C11 (160 / 154 px literals). This is informational, not a gap on its own.
